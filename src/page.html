SOUND
Music

Music is synthesised live, with drums and instruments from the sample library beside the game: Virtuosity Drums, VCSL and VSCO-2 by Versilian Studios, FreePats and the Sonic Pi pack (all CC0), and the rock kit, the MuldjordKit by Lars Muldjord (CC BY 4.0; drum samples provided by DrumGizmo.org); samples/CREDITS.md has the full list. Inside the page: drum hits and effects from the Sonic Pi sample pack (freesound.org recordings, public domain under CC0) and instruments from the Versilian Community Sample Library (CC0). Songs mode plays recorded CC0 tracks, credited on the Music page. The announcer, the spoken tabla bols and the sung chops use the open Kokoro speech model (Apache-2.0). Sound effects are public-domain (CC0) recordings from Kenney (kenney.nl), rubberduck on OpenGameArt.org and the Sonic Pi pack.

MUSIC
The genres, how the race music behaves, and your library of kept tunes · every tune has a code you can share
Mode
Genres

Each tune is a template from one genre, or a fusion of two (one genre's rhythm, another's melody or colour). A tune plays only when its genres are on. In a room everyone hears the same tunes, so there all genres play.

Like (and Keep) make tunes like that one come up more often in the menu and in races on your own; skipping a tune in its first 20 seconds counts a little against it. Nothing repeats too soon: the listening memory still comes first.

Songs

Library · the tunes you kept

Tap a name to play that tune on repeat; Back to radio ends it. Play library plays the kept tunes one after another, in the menu and in races on your own. The generator with library influence makes new tunes like them. Remove sits at the far end of each row, and Undo brings a removed tune back.
